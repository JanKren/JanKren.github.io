Paper out in _Physics of Fluids_ **37(7)**: _Capillary waves on the liquid film of a Taylor bubble in vertical countercurrent flow_ — with I. Tiselj, S. El Shawish, M. Draksler, B. Mikuž. Reports a previously uncharacterized class of waves on the Taylor bubble interface.

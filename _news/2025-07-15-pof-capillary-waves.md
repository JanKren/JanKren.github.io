Paper out in *Physics of Fluids* **37(7)**: *Capillary waves on the liquid film of a Taylor bubble in vertical countercurrent flow* — with I. Tiselj, S. El Shawish, M. Draksler, B. Mikuž. Reports a previously uncharacterized class of waves on the Taylor bubble interface.
